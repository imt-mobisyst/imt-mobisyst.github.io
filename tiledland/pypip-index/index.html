TiledLand: a Polygon-based Multi-Agents Simulation Engine.

The main idea is to model a plan world as a collection of structured convex-polygons objects. The world is mainly composed of an environnement with items and agents distributed inside it. The environment is composed by interconnected convex-polygons: Tiles.

Its is more a sandbox project to test approaches than an optimized, ready-to-use and well documented toolbox.

The documention can be found on imt-mobisyst.github.io/tiledland.
